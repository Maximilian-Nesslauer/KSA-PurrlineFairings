# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release, validated against KSA build version 2026.11.10.5801: fairing halves of 2 m and 3 m, each short, medium and long, in white paint or black carbon; fairing bases for 2 m and 3 m stacks; hammerhead bases from a 2 m stage to a 3 m fairing and from a 3 m stage to a 4 m fairing (a 3 m half at scale 1.33). Every half has a quilted acoustic blanket inside.

The 2 m medium half and the 2 m base were the fairing of Prisma by Purrline Rocketworks 1.0.0. They keep their part Ids, so vehicles that you saved with Prisma 1.0.0 load with their fairing. Prisma 1.1.0 contains the same 2 m fairing, and you can enable Prisma 1.1.0 and this mod together. Do not enable this mod together with Prisma 1.0.0: update Prisma first. Changes to these two parts: new names (Fairing Half 2 m Medium, Fairing Base 2 m), a black carbon variant of the half, the acoustic blanket, six hull colliders instead of five, and a stronger split (930 N s instead of 800 N s, so a half also clears a payload at scale 2 under thrust).
